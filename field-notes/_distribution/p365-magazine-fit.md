## Reddit post — r/P365

**Title:** Checked which mags fit which P365 grip, every SIG and Magpul mag against every factory grip. Magpul's AMAG 15 page is wrong about the XMACRO.

**Body:**

Got tired of the "does the 12 fit the XL" threads going three different ways,
so I went through every magazine listed for the P365 (nine SIG, four Magpul
AMAG, the TTI 20) against the four factory grip modules and wrote down what
each maker actually says, then checked a couple on my own guns.

The thing that sorts it all out: the grip module is the magazine housing.
The FCU and the slide have nothing to do with mag fit. Four grips, three
magazine families:

- P365 grip → Micro family
- X / XL grip (X, XL, XL Rose Comp) → XL family
- XMACRO grip (XMACRO, XMACRO Comp, AXG Legion, XF DH3, XF DH3 AXG) → Macro family
- FUSE grip (FUSE, FUSE Comp) → Macro family. Same mags as the XMACRO, longer grip.

Count of mags each maker lists as fitting, out of 14: P365 grip 11, X/XL grip
9, XMACRO grip 7, FUSE grip 5. "Fits" includes "fits with the included
baseplate" and "fits but sits extended". Extended means it hangs below the
grip line. It has nothing to do with capacity.

Two things I found that the maker pages get wrong or leave vague:

1. **Magpul AMAG 15 in the XMACRO.** Magpul's page calls it flush-fit in the
   XMACRO. That text is copy-pasted from the AMAG 17 page. On my XMACRO
   (checked 2026-10-14) the 15 sits extended with a sliver of stainless
   showing below the grip. The 17 is the flush one.
2. **SIG's 21 "seats flush in XMACRO-size grips".** That means no gap at the
   grip line, not a flush-length mag. The 21 extends below every grip it is
   listed for. The flush mag in the XMACRO, AXG and FUSE grips is the 17.

Also for anyone in a restricted state: every 10-round mag in the list (SIG
P365 10 flush, P365 10 finger ext, X/XL 10, XMACRO 10, Magpul AMAG 10) has
no state restriction. Everything above 10 is restricted in at least ten
states. Oregon's Measure 114 limit is not in effect as of 2026-10-15; it is
pending at the Oregon Supreme Court.

Aftermarket grip modules (Mischief, Icarus, ECM, Grayguns): not covered.
They take whichever family they are built for, and that is the module
maker's call. Working on a per-module version.

The full per-grip tables, with each maker's claim per mag and the restricted
states per mag, are here. Verdicts come straight from the fit rows so if a
maker changes a page I can change one row and the article changes with it:

https://gunforma.com/field-notes/p365-magazine-fit

If you have one of these mags in a grip I did not check by hand and it does
not match the maker's claim, say so and I will verify it.
