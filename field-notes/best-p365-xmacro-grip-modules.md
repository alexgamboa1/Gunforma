The grip module is the biggest ergonomic decision a [P365 XMacro](/p365/p365-xmacro) owner gets to make. On this platform the serialized part is the fire control unit; the module around it is an unserialized housing, so the texture, the weight, the magwell and the rail are all in play without touching the slide or barrel. The XMacro's housing class is also the busiest in the family: the XMacro Comp, the AXG Legion and both XF DH3 models wear the same size, the FUSE ships on it from the factory, and the 17- and 21-round magazine family feeds all of them. This page ranks every XMacro-size [grip module](/parts/frames) in the Gunforma catalog — nineteen at this review — on the catalog's own spec sheets, the makers' stated fitment and the [owner builds](/gunforma-builds.html) posted here. The short answer: the Icarus Precision MACRO A.C.E. is the strongest overall pick, and Tyrant CNC's module leads the polymer field.

## How we rank

Gunforma doesn't do hands-on reviews, and this page doesn't pretend to. The ranking is built from four things we can show you: how precisely the maker states fitment (naming the XMacro, the slide lengths, the magazine family — not just "fits P365"); how complete the module's spec sheet is in our catalog (material, weight, magwell, rail, texture); whether it can be bought today at a price our nightly retailer feed has verified; and whether it appears in real builds posted by owners, which are linked wherever they exist. Prices on this page render live on every visit — a number no feed has confirmed within the last seven days is never shown. No line below is a feeling or a reliability opinion: if it can't be traced to a spec column, a fitment row or the maker's own wording, it doesn't ship.

If you want metal with the magwell machined in rather than bolted on, this is the one the data backs.

No module in this table shows up in owners' hands more often. Wilson's full-size XMacro module undercuts even the factory module's weight — 2.40 oz against Sig's 2.50 — while adding the magwell flare and a more aggressive texture. ## Questions owners actually search

::faq
Q: Do P365 XMacro grip modules fit the FUSE?
A: The FUSE ships on the XMacro-size module, and four makers on this page state FUSE fit in their own wording: Sig (both OEM modules), Armory Craft and Mischief Machine's Commander G2. The rest are silent, which we record as unstated — not as a no.
Q: Which magazines work in an XMacro-size grip module?
A: Per Sig: the 17-round is the factory flush magazine, the XMACRO 10-round sits flush, the 21-round extends below the grip, and the X/XL 10-, 12- and 15-round magazines are listed as not compatible.
Q: Do XMacro grip modules work with P365 XL slides?
A: Where the maker says: Icarus states its modules fit XL (3.7") or XMACRO (3.1") slides, and Sig names an XL- or XMACRO-length slide assembly for its module. Wilson notes XL slides protrude past its Short module's dust cover. Where a maker is silent, this site doesn't guess.
Q: What's the difference between Mischief Machine's XMacro and XXL frames?
A: Per Mischief, the Macro frame matches the OEM X-Macro and the Fuse in size, while the XXL also takes the 12-round flush magazines and carries a rail that sits flush with a 3.7" slide.
Q: Is swapping a grip module a gunsmith job?
A: On the P365 the serialized part is the fire control unit; the grip module is an unserialized housing the FCU moves into. Follow the maker's instructions, and check your own state's rules on configurations and magazines — this page doesn't give legal advice.
Q: Does a new grip module change holster fit?
A: It can. A module swap doesn't touch the slide, but dust-cover length and rail type vary across this table — Wilson's Short module carries a proprietary rail, and Wilson notes XL slides protrude past its dust cover — so check your holster maker's listed fits, especially with a light mounted.
::
