On the P365 platform the grip module decides which magazines fit. The
serialized fire control unit and the slide have no say in it. The 11 P365
models in the catalog ship with four grip modules, and the 14 magazines in
the catalog carry 48 maker fit claims across them: 11 magazines are listed
as fitting the P365 grip, 9 the P365 X / XL grip, 7 the XMacro-family grip
and 5 the FUSE grip. Every 10-round magazine in the catalog carries no state
restriction; every magazine above 10 rounds is restricted in at least ten
states.

Every verdict on this page is rendered from the maker's own fit claim for
that grip module, read from the catalog on each visit. **Extended** means
the magazine sits below the grip line. It says nothing about capacity, which
is shown on its own on every row. A magazine with no maker claim for a grip
module is not listed under it.

## Your grip module decides your mag fit

On the P365 the serialized part is the fire control unit. The grip module is
a separate part, and it is the magazine housing. So magazine compatibility
follows the grip module, not the slide and not the FCU. The catalog records
it as a grip module's housing class and the magazine family that class takes:

| Grip module | Factory models that ship with it | Magazine family |
|---|---|---|
| P365 | P365 | Micro |
| P365 X / XL | P365 X, P365 XL, P365 XL Rose Comp | XL |
| P365 XMACRO | XMacro, XMacro Comp, AXG Legion, XF DH3, XF DH3 AXG | Macro |
| P365 FUSE | FUSE, FUSE Comp | Macro |

Two things follow. Swap the grip module and the magazines that fit are the
new module's, whatever the box said. And the XMacro and the FUSE take the
same magazines even though the FUSE grip is longer.

This page covers the factory grip modules. Aftermarket grip modules
(Mischief Machine, Icarus Precision, ECM Precision, Grayguns and others)
take whichever magazine family they are built for; check the maker's page
for now. A per-module fit guide is coming.

## State capacity limits

The "Not available in" line on a row above is the catalog's restricted-state
list for that magazine. Each list was cross-checked against these limits on
2026-10-15:

- **10-round limit:** CA, CT, DC, HI (handguns), MA, MD, NJ, NY, RI, WA.
- **15-round limit:** CO; IL and VT (handguns).
- **17-round limit:** DE.
- **Oregon:** Measure 114's 10-round limit is not in effect. It is pending
  at the Oregon Supreme Court, and HB 4145 would delay it to 2027. Checked
  2026-10-15; no row lists Oregon.

Five catalog magazines carry no restricted states, and all five hold 10
rounds: SIG's P365 10-round flush and 10-round finger-extension magazines,
SIG's X/XL 10-round, SIG's XMacro 10-round, and Magpul's AMAG 10. Which of
them fits is decided by the grip module, in the sections above.

Capacity laws change. The lists above were verified as of the date shown.
This is not legal advice.

## Corrections worth knowing

Two maker pages say something the magazine does not do. The rows above
record what was verified, not what the page says.

**Magpul AMAG 15 in the XMacro.** Magpul's product page calls the AMAG 15
flush-fit in the XMacro, and the text is copy-pasted from the AMAG 17 page.
I checked it on 2026-10-14: the 15 sits extended in the XMacro with a sliver
of stainless showing.

**Magpul AMAG 12 in the X / XL grip.** Magpul lists the AMAG 12 for the X and
XL grips and does not say how it sits. I checked it on 2026-10-14: flush,
with the included X/XL floorplate.

**SIG's 21-round and "seats flush".** SIG's page for the 21-round magazine
says it seats flush in XMacro-size grips. That means no gap at the grip
line, not a flush-length magazine: the 21 extends below every grip it is
listed for, and in the XMacro its baseplate meets the grip with no gap. The
flush magazine in the XMacro, AXG and FUSE grips is the 17-round. Reading
documented 2026-10-14.

## Questions people ask

### Do P365 XL mags fit the regular P365?

SIG ships the X/XL 12-round and 15-round magazines with a second baseplate
for the standard P365 grip, and lists the X/XL 10-round as not recommended
in it. The [P365](#p365) section has the row for each one.

### Are Sig P365 magazines interchangeable?

Not across the line. The grip module decides: the P365 grip takes the Micro
family, the X / XL grip the XL family, and the XMacro and FUSE grips the
Macro family. Where a magazine from one family is listed for another grip,
its row says how it sits and whether an included part is needed.

### What magazines fit the P365 XMacro?

The makers list 7 of the 14 catalog magazines as fitting the XMacro grip
module, and 4 of those sit flush. The [P365 XMacro](#p365-xmacro) section
shows each one, and the same rows apply to the XMacro Comp, AXG Legion,
XF DH3 and XF DH3 AXG.

### Does the 17-round mag fit the P365 XL?

SIG lists the 17-round for the X and XL with its included X/XL floorplate,
extending below the grip. The [P365 X](#p365-x) section has the row.

### What is the highest-capacity CA-legal P365 magazine?

Ten rounds. California limits detachable magazines to 10 rounds, and the
five 10-round magazines in the catalog carry no restricted states. Pick by
grip module from the sections above.

### Do P365 mags fit aftermarket grip modules?

The grip module is the magazine housing, so an aftermarket module takes
whichever magazine family it is built for, and that is the module maker's
call. This page covers the factory modules only; a per-module fit guide is
coming.

### What mags fit the P365 FUSE?

The FUSE takes the Macro family, the same as the XMacro. The makers list 5
of the catalog magazines for it, and the catalog holds no Magpul claim for
the FUSE grip. The [P365 FUSE](#p365-fuse) section has each row.
