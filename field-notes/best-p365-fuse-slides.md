The Gunforma catalog lists 12 aftermarket P365 FUSE slides from Arc Division, Fdez Werx, Norsso and True Precision. Each is a 4.3" slide for a 4.3" barrel and the P365-FUSE recoil spring. The barrel sets them apart: four plain slides take threaded barrels, seven ported slides take a matched ported barrel, and one kit includes its own.

## Will a P365 FUSE slide fit a P365, XL or XMacro frame? {toc="Frame fit"}

Yes. Every P365 FUSE slide in the Gunforma catalog fits every P365 grip size, because the slide rides on the fire control unit, not the grip. What the grip decides is where the slide ends: on a FUSE-length grip the slide runs flush with the front of the grip, and on a P365, X/XL or XMacro grip the 4.3" slide extends past it.

The factory P365 FUSE ships on the XMacro-size grip, so a FUSE slide on a stock FUSE extends past the grip the same way the factory slide does. A FUSE-length aftermarket grip is what makes it flush.

The grip is half of it. On any of those grips, the slide still takes a 4.3" FUSE barrel and the P365-FUSE recoil spring assembly: the spring follows slide length, so an XL or XMacro owner moving to a FUSE slide changes the barrel and the spring with it. Per True Precision, the True Precision P365-FUSE fits P365 (3.1") and P365XL (3.7") FCUs "with the correct barrel and recoil spring." Per Fdez Werx, its three FUSE slides fit "all P365 FCUs and grip modules."

## Plain or ported: which P365 FUSE slide do you need?

All 12 are 4.3" slides for a 4.3" barrel and the P365-FUSE recoil spring assembly. The choice that splits them is the barrel, and it sorts them into five groups:

| Group | Slides | Port style | Built around | Takes a threaded barrel | Barrel included |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain | True Precision, Norsso Barracuda EDC, Rail EDC, Reptile EDC | None | A threaded or flush 4.3" FUSE barrel | Yes | No |
| Fdez Werx ported | 365RS, ACCS, P365 FUSE 4.3" | Top & Chunk (top + front) | Fdez Werx FUSE ported or chunk port barrel | Yes | No |
| Norsso C-Port | Piranha C, Reptile C | C-Port (two openings) | Norsso N365-FUSE C Port barrel | Flush barrels only | No |
| Norsso E Port | Rail KP, Reptile KP | E Port (straight port) | Norsso N365-FUSE E Port barrel | Flush barrels only | No |
| Arc Division kit | Reactor 365F | Chunk Port (front port) | Its own barrel, in the kit | Flush barrels only | Yes |

A ported slide vents gas through openings that line up with its matched ported barrel. Each ported slide in the Gunforma catalog also takes a flush 4.3" FUSE barrel, which fits with no comp effect. The plain slides and the Fdez Werx slides are the ones that take a threaded barrel, for a muzzle device or a suppressor.

## Standard and threaded-barrel P365 FUSE slides

These four are unported, and they take a threaded or flush 4.3" FUSE barrel.

::rank true-precision-p365-fuse best="Unported, lightening cuts, more than one color" rank=1
= Unported, like the factory P365 FUSE slide
::

The True Precision P365-FUSE is the only slide in this group with lightening cuts, and the only FUSE slide in the Gunforma catalog offered in more than one color. It is 416R stainless. Per True Precision, it also fits P365 and P365XL FCUs with the correct barrel and recoil spring.

::rank norsso-barracuda-edc-p365-fuse best="Unported, 17-4 stainless" rank=2
= Unported, like the factory P365 FUSE slide
::

The Norsso P365-FUSE Barracuda EDC is a 17-4 stainless slide for P365-FUSE 4.3" barrels. Per Norsso, "Threaded barrels are compatible."

::rank norsso-rail-edc-p365-fuse best="Unported, 17-4 stainless" rank=3
= Unported, like the factory P365 FUSE slide
::

The Norsso P365-FUSE Rail EDC carries the same spec values in the Gunforma catalog as the Barracuda EDC and the Reptile EDC; the three names are Norsso's three slide designs. It takes threaded and flush 4.3" FUSE barrels.

::rank norsso-reptile-edc-p365-fuse best="Unported, 17-4 stainless" rank=4
= Unported, like the factory P365 FUSE slide
::

The Norsso P365-FUSE Reptile EDC is the unported Reptile; the Gunforma catalog also lists the Reptile C and Reptile KP, both ported. Per Norsso, it takes P365-FUSE 4.3" barrels, threaded included.

FUSE Comp and XF DH3 owners run a 3.7" barrel; the barrels here are 4.3", for the standard FUSE slide. For each barrel's specs, see [the P365 FUSE barrels field note](/field-notes/best-p365-fuse-threaded-barrels).

## Compensated P365 FUSE slides and their matched barrels

A ported slide's openings are cut to line up with one maker's ported barrel, which is why each system below names its barrel. Every slide here also takes a flush 4.3" FUSE barrel, which fits with no comp effect.

The three Fdez Werx slides are built around the Fdez Werx FUSE ported barrel and its chunk port barrel, and they also take threaded barrels.

::rank fdez-werx-accs-p365-fuse best="Top & Chunk ports, lightening cuts" rank=1

The Fdez Werx P365 FUSE ACCS 4.3" Slide is 416 stainless with lightening cuts. Per Fdez Werx, it fits all P365 FCUs and grip modules.

~ Top & Chunk ports (top + front); the factory P365 FUSE slide is unported

## FAQ

::faq
Q: How long is a P365 FUSE slide?
A: Every P365 FUSE slide in the Gunforma catalog is 4.3". It takes a 4.3" FUSE barrel and the P365-FUSE recoil spring assembly.
Q: Will a P365 FUSE slide fit a P365 XL frame?
A: Yes. Every P365 FUSE slide in the Gunforma catalog fits the P365 X/XL grip, extending past the front of it, with a 4.3" FUSE barrel and the P365-FUSE recoil spring assembly.
Q: Will a P365 FUSE slide fit an XMacro frame?
A: Yes. It extends past the XMacro grip, the same way the factory P365 FUSE slide does, because the factory FUSE ships on the XMacro-size grip.
Q: Will a P365 FUSE slide fit a standard P365 frame?
A: Yes. All 12 fit the original P365 grip, extending past it, with a 4.3" FUSE barrel and the P365-FUSE recoil spring assembly.
Q: Which P365 FUSE slides take a threaded barrel?
A: Seven: the True Precision P365-FUSE, the Norsso Barracuda, Rail and Reptile EDC, and the Fdez Werx 365RS, ACCS and P365 FUSE 4.3". The other five take their matched ported barrel or a flush 4.3" FUSE barrel.
Q: P365 XL slide vs FUSE slide: what changes?
A: The XL slide is 3.7" and takes a 3.7" barrel and the XL-length spring; the FUSE slide is 4.3" and takes a 4.3" barrel and the P365-FUSE recoil spring assembly. Both fit the same P365 FCU.
Q: Does a P365 FUSE slide come with a barrel?
A: The Arc Division Reactor 365F ships as a slide and barrel kit. The other eleven take a 4.3" FUSE barrel bought separately.
Q: Do these slides work on a P365 FUSE Comp?
A: Yes, with a 4.3" FUSE barrel. The FUSE Comp's stock barrel is 3.7", and these 12 slides take 4.3" barrels.
::
