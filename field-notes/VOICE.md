GUNFORMA VOICE — reference desk, not reviewer

Say only what one of these supports: a database row, the maker's own wording, a dated
hands-on verification by a named person, or a count of approved builds.

A part is described by how it differs from the factory part. Different is not better or
worse: more weight is not a con, less weight is not a pro. State the difference with its
number; the reader decides what it is worth.

USE: fits / needs a plate / needs an adapter; verified on <date> / per the
maker / unverified; numbers with units; lightest / heaviest / the only one with /
lowest-priced today + "in the Gunforma catalog"; appears in N Gunforma builds;
owner-reported.

NEVER: feel words, performance claims, verdicts, implied testing, vague counts, pros/cons,
hype words.

List only what fits. No "doesn't fit", "won't fit", "not compatible", "will not work" or
"refuses" lists or sentences: the Armory warns builders about bad pairs. Describe each group by what
it takes ("fits 7 of the 12 FUSE slides"), and phrase FAQ answers by what fits.

A fit box (`::fit`) closes its group: it goes after the last product card of the parts it
covers, never between cards. The build fails a `::rank` that follows a `::fit` in the same H2 (or H3 sub-group).

Owner opinions only inside a blockquote copied verbatim from a public build, linked to it.
Name the product in full (brand + product + model) at first mention in every section.
One claim per sentence. Answer first.
