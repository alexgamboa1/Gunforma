Methods

How Gunforma checks parts

Gunforma is a fitment catalog. We don't review or range-test parts. We record what each part is, what it fits, and what it costs today.

Sources

Where the specs come from

Specs are taken from the maker's own product page. Where a retailer's description disagrees with the maker, the maker wins. Where nobody publishes a value, we leave it blank instead of guessing.

Fitment

How fit is decided

Fit comes from the parts themselves: a slide's length against a grip module's, an optic's footprint against a slide's cut, a light's rail against a grip module's rail. When a maker says a part fits or doesn't, that statement is recorded.

Confidence

Verified and likely

"Verified" means a person mounted that part on that part, or an owner reported it in a Gunforma build. "Likely" means the specs say it should fit and no one has confirmed it yet. Every fit table shows which is which.

Prices

Prices

Prices come from partner retailers and refresh automatically. A price we can't confirm is not shown. Some links earn Gunforma a commission; that never changes what fits.

Corrections

Corrections

When a fact on Gunforma turns out to be wrong, we fix it at the source, and articles that used it say so in their update log. Found something wrong? Reach us at contact@gunforma.com.
