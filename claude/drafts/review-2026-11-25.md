## Data gaps (catalog fixes, not article fixes)

- `weight_oz` is null on all 12 slides, so the cards show no weight row (pinned by `fit-slide-block.test.mjs`).
- There's no optic-cut column for slides, so the note states no cut, except Arc's, which is quoted from its maker note.
- None of the 12 slides is in an approved build, so no card shows "Used in N builds".
- Parked: the two Fdez Werx Gunforma guides ("P365 Fuse Competition", "P365 FUSE turned into a 2011 GTO") are left out until their optic rows are fixed. The Romeo3 Pro and the 507C X3 have no fit row on those slides' cuts.
- Parked: the `integrated_comp` flag isn't used. Ported slides are described by `port_style` and the matched barrel.
