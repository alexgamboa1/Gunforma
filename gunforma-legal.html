Legal

Informational platform, not an FFL

Gunforma is an informational and community platform. We are not a Federal Firearms Licensee (FFL). We do not sell, ship, transfer, or facilitate the transfer of firearms, firearm frames, receivers, or restricted parts. Everything on this site is user-submitted content and product reference material — not an offer to sell.

Firearm laws — including restrictions on magazine capacity, barrel and overall length, optics and light mounting, suppressors, short-barreled configurations, and dealer transfer requirements — vary by federal, state, and local jurisdiction, and change often. A build shown here may not be legal to own, buy, ship, or configure the same way where you live.

You are solely responsible for compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws before purchasing, assembling, shipping, or modifying anything referenced on this site. When in doubt, consult your local FFL or an attorney.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links pay us a commission

Some product links on Gunforma are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, Gunforma may earn a commission from the retailer — at no additional cost to you. This helps fund the site. Affiliate relationships never determine which parts appear in a build or how a product is described; users pick the parts, and the commission (if any) is a byproduct of showing you where to buy them.

Community guidelines

What belongs in a build post

  • Builds must be real. Photos of your actual pistol — not renders, mockups, or CAD screenshots.
  • No illegal modifications. No auto sears, no unregistered SBRs, no unregistered suppressors, no anything that would land you in federal court. Not on this platform.
  • No serial numbers in photos. Cover, blur, or angle around them before uploading. Protect your own OPSEC.
  • Accurate parts. List what's actually on the gun. Don't tag a part you didn't use to farm affiliate clicks or to get a build past review faster.
  • Respect the community. Disagree with a build choice? Fine. Attack the builder? Not fine. Keep comments constructive, keep it civil.
  • Yours to share. Only upload photos you took or have the right to use. Don't repost other people's builds as your own.
